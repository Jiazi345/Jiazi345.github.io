为了方便获取光源的视角作为摄像机渲染的参数，用了custom render pipleline

https://github.com/w199753/SRP_GI_ImpTest/blob/main/Assets/Scripts/FRP.cs

https://catlikecoding.com/unity/tutorials/custom-srp/custom-render-pipeline/


流程：

-cull

CullingResults cullResults= context.Cull(ref ScriptableCullingParameters p)

-清除render target

buffer.ClearRenderTarget(true,true,Color.clear);

-设置 context操作

context.SetupCameraProperties(camera);

context.DrawRnederers(cullingresults,drawSettings, SortSettings);

context.DrawSkybox(camera);

...

-提交command buffer 到 context 并清除buffer以供下次使用

context.ExcuteCommandBuffer(buffer);

buffer.clear();

-提交context

context.submit();


错题本：

替换自定义CustomRenderPipeline Assets到Graphic中，未生效- 在Quality中选择的RenderPilelineAssets仍为URP
